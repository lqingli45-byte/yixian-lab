About

Start from real life, and keep what works.

Yixian Lab (易娴实验室) is a personal content brand that also offers practical digital products. It corresponds to the Chinese publication 《易晓娴·成长实验》.

The brand

Yixian Lab is run by a writer living in China. The work begins with observing, recording, and using AI tools in everyday life, then turning useful questions and methods into writing people can read and try. 《易晓娴·成长实验》 is the main publishing channel. This website is the public home of the brand, and it also describes the completed first product and later planned directions.

What we write about

The publication covers personal growth, parent-child communication, emotional understanding, AI productivity, and making knowledge usable. The website and publication do not publish family privacy, and they do not describe unfinished work or unsold products as if they were already available.

Digital products

Our first digital product is the AI Work Log & Review Kit (v1.1), a US$5 one-time download with templates for keeping and reviewing work records.
